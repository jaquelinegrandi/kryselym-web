About Us

Publishing with purpose

Kryselum Editorial was created around a simple belief: professional publishing should never require an author to give up ownership of their work..

01 — Who we are

A different kind of publishing house

Kryselum Editorial is an independent publishing house built around an author-first model.

We invest in the professional production of the books we publish while allowing authors to remain the owners of their work.

Our role is not to take control of a story, but to provide the expertise, resources, and care needed to help it reach readers.

We believe professional publishing and author independence should be able to exist together.

03 — Our approach

Professional publishing without giving up ownership.

Kryselum does not charge authors for the standard publishing production of an accepted manuscript.

We invest our time, expertise, and resources into every book we choose to publish. Because every publication represents a real investment from Kryselum, we deliberately limit the number of books we accept each year.

This allows us to give every accepted title the attention, editorial care, and production quality it deserves.

You retain your copyright.

You keep 100% of your book royalties.

You control your publishing accounts.

You own your intellectual property.

Acceptance is based on the quality and suitability of the manuscript, never on the purchase of additional services.

04 — What we offer

Professional publishing, without the cost to the author.

When Kryselum accepts a manuscript, we invest in its editorial production. Authors do not pay for the standard publishing package, retain their rights, and keep their publishing royalties.

Our standard publishing support includes the work required to prepare a book for publication and introduce it to the Kryselum catalogue.

Included with publication

Every accepted author receives:

  • Editing and proofreading.
  • Professional cover design.
  • ISBN under the Kryselum imprint.
  • Metadata preparation.
  • Publishing guidance.
  • Inclusion in the Kryselum catalogue.
  • Support with ARC readers.

Initial visibility

Publication also includes a basic level of promotional support designed to give each new title an initial presence.

  • Inclusion on the Kryselum blog.
  • A social media feature through Kryselum's channels.
  • Basic Kolen marketing support for the author.

Additional support is available for authors who wish to develop their visibility further, including expanded Kolen packages, author websites, SEO, and other promotional opportunities.

06 — Kryselum Ecosystem

More than publishing.

Kryselum Editorial forms part of a wider ecosystem created to support authors at different stages of their professional journey, both before and beyond publication.

Theruma

Professional book formatting and publication preparation for authors who want their manuscripts prepared to professional publishing standards.

Kryselum Webs

Professional author websites designed to give an author's work a dedicated and lasting presence online.

Authors publishing with Kryselum may receive the initial website setup at no additional cost. Ongoing hosting and maintenance are provided through a monthly service.

Kolen

Author branding and marketing support created to help authors become known, build their audience, and develop their professional presence.

07 — What we believe

Protect rather than possess.

We believe a publisher's role is not to take ownership of a story, but to help it become the best version of itself.

To protect rather than possess.

To guide rather than control.

To invest rather than exploit.

To preserve rather than simply produce.

Publishing is not merely the act of making books available. It is the responsibility of guiding stories towards something lasting.

08 — Our commitment

Built on trust. Sustained by quality.

Every manuscript accepted by Kryselum Editorial represents a mutual commitment.

We commit our professional expertise, editorial resources, and production investment to the work.

In return, we ask for trust, collaboration, and a shared commitment to producing work of lasting quality.

Our success is not measured by the number of books we publish, but by the quality of the stories we help bring into the world and the relationships we build with the authors who create them.
